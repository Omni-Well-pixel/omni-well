How I coach

Assess. Develop. Prepare. Perform.

1. Assess

Understand the athlete's training history, performance, goals and competition requirements.

2. Develop

Improve strength, power, aerobic capacity and repeat-effort conditioning.

3. Prepare

Adapt programming to discipline, weight class, training load and competition schedule.

4. Perform

Track progress, manage fatigue, retest and prepare appropriately for competition.

How the week looks

The standard development programme uses two strength/power sessions and two dedicated conditioning sessions each week, adjusted around technical training and sparring.

Includes athlete onboarding and performance assessment, strength and explosive power work, aerobic and anaerobic conditioning, fight preparation and recovery monitoring.
